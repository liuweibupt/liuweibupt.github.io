I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Integrated Circuits, Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Hu He in DSPLab. My research focuses on GPGPU architecture, tensor-core microarchitecture, AI infrastructure, and hardware/software co-design for machine-learning workloads.

我目前主要参与清华大学“乘影”RISC-V 开源 GPGPU 项目，研究面向 AI 负载的 GPGPU 体系结构、可复用多精度 Tensor Core、Tensor Core 数值精度建模以及多精度 SFU 设计。

## Research Interests

- GPGPU architecture and SIMT design-space exploration
- Multi-precision Tensor Core / SFU microarchitecture
- Numerical effects of tensor-core microarchitecture in training and inference
- ML systems, model compression, and hardware/software co-design
- Remote sensing image and LiDAR point-cloud semantic segmentation

## Selected Highlights

- **TensorForge**: designed a reusable multi-precision Tensor Core supporting TF32, BF16, FP16, INT8/4, FP8/6/4, and Binary formats; integrated into Ventus GPGPU.
- **TensorGauge**: built a CUDA-accelerated bit-level Tensor Core numerical model and integrated it with PyTorch for pre-silicon end-to-end evaluation.
- **DriveLM compression**: optimized multimodal vision-language model inference through AWQ/PTQ, LoRA fusion, and FP16 Tensor Core acceleration.
- **DLAFNet**: published 2D aerial image and 3D LiDAR point-cloud fusion work in IGARSS 2023 and IEEE JSTARS 2025.
