How to read a query

M × N is the dot-product unit count; K is the dot-product length. MX/NV, normalization, accumulator, output, and midMat options retain the configuration fields from the release.

  • Measured Array: a matching synthesized array; displayed per-unit values divide its area and power by M × N.
  • Derived from Measured Unit: array area and power equal M × N times the synthesized unit values; array interconnect and integration overhead are not modeled.
  • Predicted Unit: a released, precomputed cost-model estimate, scaled by M × N. Unavailable estimates are not generated on demand. Disable prediction fallback to restrict results to measured arrays or measured-unit derivations.

Selecting a combination does not guarantee a measured result. The result type and formulas below show which data support each answer.

About the data

        
TensorForge explores reusable multi-precision Tensor Core microarchitectures for GPGPUs. This explorer presents the released synthesis datasets and follows the original query behavior.

        
“Measured” refers to synthesis reports from Design Compiler under TSMC 22 nm assumptions, rather than measurements from fabricated silicon. Derived arrays scale a synthesized dot-product unit by M × N; predicted units use precomputed estimates from the released cost model.
