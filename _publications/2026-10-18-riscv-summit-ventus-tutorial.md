Wei Liu spoke in the Ventus open-source GPGPU tutorial at RISC-V Summit China in Shenzhen on October 18, 2026. The talk covered how to evaluate the effect of accumulation precision on models with TensorGauge and how to explore hardware reuse strategies for multi-precision tensor cores with TensorForge.
